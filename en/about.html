About Bawabat Granada Trading & Maintenance Est.

Company Profile

Reliable Technical Excellence for Homes and Commercial Facilities

Bawabat Granada Trading Est. specializes in providing comprehensive maintenance and technical works for homes and facilities in Riyadh, including plumbing, electrical, painting, decoration, and home renovation.

We provide installation and repair of water supply and drainage networks, water leak detection, sewer unblocking, installation of sanitary fixtures and water heaters, execution and maintenance of electrical works, lighting, switches and sockets, as well as interior and exterior painting, crack repair, and space renovation.

We rely on specialized and experienced technicians, ensuring the use of proper tools and materials, with a firm commitment to quality, precision, quick response, and on-time completion. Our goal is to deliver practical, dependable solutions that fulfill customer requirements while preserving safety and comfort.
